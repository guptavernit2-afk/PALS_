    About CatchUp

    Built for the PROTOCOL X hackathon by team PALS_.

    What it does

    CatchUp uses a genuine pretrained AI model (Llama 3.2 1B) running entirely in your browser via WebLLM/WebGPU. Paste any conversation and get an instant breakdown of what needs your attention, action items, decisions, and open questions.

    Privacy first

    Your conversation text never leaves your device. The AI model runs locally using your GPU. Only the model weights are downloaded once and cached — no conversation data is ever transmitted to any server.

    How it works

    1. Paste or load a sample conversation
    2. The AI model initializes in your browser (first time takes ~30s)
    3. Analysis runs entirely on your device's GPU
    4. Results are parsed, validated against source messages, and displayed
